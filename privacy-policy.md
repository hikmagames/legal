# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: December 21, 2024**

## Introduction

Hikma Games ("we," "our," or "us") operates the TasbeehTaps mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our App.

## Information We Collect

### Information We Do NOT Collect

TasbeehTaps is designed with your privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. Specifically, we do not collect:

- Names, email addresses, or contact information
- Location data
- Device identifiers or advertising IDs
- Usage analytics or tracking data
- Photos, media, or files
- Financial or payment information

### Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores the following data **locally on your device only**:

- Your dhikr (remembrance) counts and history
- App preferences (sound, vibration, theme, language settings)
- Game progress and statistics

This data:
- Never leaves your device
- Is not transmitted to any server
- Is not accessible to us or any third party
- Can be deleted by uninstalling the App or clearing App data

## Third-Party Services

### External Links

The App contains optional links to external services:

- **Buy Me a Coffee** - For voluntary donations
- **Patreon** - For voluntary donations
- **Google Play Store** - For app ratings
- **Email** - For contacting us

When you click these links, you leave our App and are subject to the privacy policies of those respective services. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.

### No Ads or Analytics

TasbeehTaps does not contain:
- Advertisements
- Analytics services
- Crash reporting services
- Any third-party tracking SDKs

## Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device and we do not collect any personal information, there is no data transmission that requires protection. Your local data is protected by your device's security features.

## Children's Privacy

TasbeehTaps does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The App is safe for users of all ages.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.

## Your Rights

Since we do not collect any personal data, there is no personal data to access, modify, or delete from our systems. You can delete all locally stored data by:
- Clearing the App's data in your device settings, or
- Uninstalling the App
