Privacy Policy - Tasbeeh: Islamic Memory Game
Effective date: 3 August 2026

Hikma Games ("we", "us") built the Tasbeeh: Islamic Memory Game app (the "App"). This policy explains what information the App handles and how. By using the App you agree to this policy.

We designed the App to collect as little as possible. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email, or any personal profile.

Information stored on your device
Your game data, progress, level, lives, daily dhikr counts, statistics, your chosen dhikr type, and your selected prayer-time city is stored locally on your device only. We do not upload, transmit, or have access to this information. Removing the App deletes it.

Location
The App does not access your device's GPS or location. Prayer times are calculated on your device from a city that you choose manually in Settings. That choice stays on your device.

Advertising
The free version of the App shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, and app-usage data. This processing is governed by Google's own policies:

How Google uses information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
You can limit ad personalization in your device settings. Purchasing Remove Ads stops ads from being shown.

Purchases
The App offers a one-time Remove Ads purchase. Payments are processed entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never receive or store your payment details. Your purchase is tied to your store account so it can be restored on your devices.

Children
The App is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the free version displays ads served by Google, we encourage parents to supervise use and, if desired, use the ad-free (purchased) version.

Data security & retention
Because game data lives only on your device, its security depends on your device. We retain no personal data on our servers. Ad and purchase data are retained by Google and Apple according to their own policies, linked above.

Changes to this policy
We may update this policy as the App evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a new effective date.
